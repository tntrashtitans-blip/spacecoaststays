The Quick Breakdown

What You Need to Know

The Complex

USSSA Space Coast Complex

12 championship fields, pro-level amenities, snack bar, and full facilities on site. One of the highest-rated tournament venues in the Southeast, running events from youth rec leagues up through competitive travel ball.

5099 Gleason Pkwy · Viera, FL 32940 ↗

The Drive

20-25 Minutes Each Way

Sandcastles to the complex via FL-528 West to I-95 South, then Wickham Road. All highway — no lights, no stress. Most games start at 8 AM, which means you're leaving at 7:30, not 6:45. That half hour matters on tournament weekends.

Book Smart

Reserve 60-90 Days Out

Tournament weekends fill fast across all of Brevard County. Condos go sooner than hotels because a group of four to six families can share one unit — so there are fewer units to go around. For peak spring and fall tournament weekends, 60-90 days out is not early.

Beyond Viera

Other Tournament Venues Nearby

The USSSA Space Coast Complex draws the most out-of-town tournament traffic, but Brevard County has a strong network of athletic facilities that host tournaments throughout the year. Sandcastles is a solid home base for all of them.

  • 1

    Viera Regional Community Park

    Adjacent to Space Coast Complex in Viera — additional fields and athletic facilities that frequently host overflow games and smaller tournaments on the same tournament weekends.

  • 2

    Cocoa Beach / Patrick SFB Fields

    Local athletic fields in Cocoa Beach and on Patrick Space Force Base host smaller local tournaments and league play — sometimes walkable from Sandcastles depending on the event.

  • 3

    Melbourne Athletic Complex

    A well-equipped complex in Melbourne, roughly 30 minutes south of Cocoa Beach via A1A or US-1, hosting a range of youth and adult athletic tournaments throughout the year.

  • 4

    Space Coast Stadium (Viera)

    The former spring training home in Viera is occasionally activated for large-scale events and tournaments. About the same drive from Sandcastles as Space Coast Complex — 20-25 minutes via I-95.

When You're Not Playing

The Space Coast Is a Destination, Not Just a Venue

The teams that come back every year aren't just coming for the tournament. They're coming for everything else too.

Kennedy Space Center

Just 12 miles north of Sandcastles — walk through a Saturn V rocket, watch a live launch, and meet a real astronaut. KSC is one of the most impressive experiences in Florida, full stop. Plan a few hours on a rest day or after early morning games wrap up.

Rocket Launches

SpaceX launches Falcon 9s and Falcon Heavies from Cape Canaveral dozens of times a year. If a launch falls during your tournament weekend, you can watch it from the beach, from your balcony, or from Jetty Park just 6 miles up the road. It never gets old.

The Atlantic Beach

The ocean is the reason families tell their kids about this tournament trip for years. After games, hit the water. Rent a surfboard, throw a frisbee, or just sit in the sand while the sun goes down over the Indian River. Cocoa Beach delivers.

Port Canaveral

Six miles north of Sandcastles — cruise ships, waterfront restaurants, fishing charters, and dolphin tours. Rusty's and Grills are packed on tournament weekends because every other team found the same idea. Get there early or late.

Ron Jon and the Boardwalk

The world's largest surf shop is open 24 hours and is a must-stop, even if you don't buy anything. Walk down N Atlantic Ave, grab a meal, and let the kids burn off energy before the next game day.

Real Food, Not Hotel Takeout

Coconuts on the Beach for frozen drinks on the sand, The Fat Snook for the best dinner in town, Juice N Java for breakfast before the first game. Your kitchen handles the rest — full grocery stores are five minutes away.

Common Questions

Tournament Lodging FAQ

How far is Sandcastles Condominiums from USSSA Space Coast Complex? +
Sandcastles Condominiums at 1000 N Atlantic Ave, Cocoa Beach is approximately 20-25 miles from USSSA Space Coast Complex at 5099 Gleason Pkwy in Viera. The drive takes about 20-25 minutes via FL-528 West to I-95 South, then Wickham Road — all highway, no traffic lights, and very manageable at 7:30 in the morning before an 8 AM first pitch.
Are condos available for tournament weekends? +
Yes — units at Sandcastles are available year-round, including tournament weekends. That said, availability moves fast. For peak spring and fall tournament season (March through May, September through November), we strongly recommend booking 60-90 days in advance. Units are listed and booked directly through owners on the Sandcastles page.
Is there enough space for multiple families traveling together? +
Yes. Full 2-bedroom, 2-bathroom units at Sandcastles comfortably sleep 6-8 people. For larger groups traveling with multiple families, several units can often be booked in proximity to each other. Compared to booking separate hotel rooms, a shared condo is significantly more comfortable and cost-effective — and you get a full kitchen and laundry.
Is there a pool at Sandcastles? +
Yes — Sandcastles has a heated outdoor pool and hot tub. After a full day of tournament games in the Florida heat, the pool is one of the most popular amenities with tournament families. The beach is also directly accessible from the building, just steps across the sand.
What tournaments are held at USSSA Space Coast Complex? +
USSSA Space Coast Complex hosts tournaments from USSSA, NSA, ASA/USA Softball, Perfect Game, and a range of independent tournament organizations. Events run year-round with the heaviest schedule from March through November — covering baseball, softball, and other field sports at youth, travel ball, and adult competitive levels.
