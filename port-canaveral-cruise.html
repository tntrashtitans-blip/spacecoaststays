Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

How far is Sandcastles from the Port Canaveral cruise terminal?

Sandcastles Condominiums at 1000 N Atlantic Ave, Cocoa Beach is approximately 5 miles south of the Port Canaveral cruise terminals — a straight shot north up A1A with no highway or interchange required. An Uber or Lyft typically takes 10 minutes and costs $10–$15 each way. There is no shuttle needed. On embarkation morning, you can sleep in, have coffee on your balcony, and still make it to the terminal well ahead of any boarding time.

Is there parking at Sandcastles Condominiums?

Yes — Sandcastles has free surface parking for guests. For a 7-night cruise, you can leave your car parked at the condo the entire time and Uber to the terminal on departure day. That's a far better arrangement than paying $20–$25 per day at the port's own parking garages — a week of port parking runs $140–$175, more than a night at Sandcastles. Uber back when you return, clear your bags, and use the afternoon at the pool or on the beach before driving home. Park your car once. Done.

Which cruise lines sail from Port Canaveral?

Five major cruise lines operate year-round from Port Canaveral: Carnival Cruise Line, Royal Caribbean International, Disney Cruise Line, Norwegian Cruise Line, and MSC Cruises. Each line offers multiple sailings per week. Itineraries range from 3-night Bahamas getaways to 9-night Eastern and Western Caribbean sailings. Port Canaveral is the second-busiest cruise port in the United States, processing more than 5 million passengers annually.

What restaurants are near Port Canaveral?

The Port Canaveral south waterfront has two standout options: Grills Seafood Deck and Rusty's Seafood & Oyster Bar. Both sit directly on the port's south bank with outdoor decks overlooking the cruise channel. Expect fresh-caught seafood, cold drinks, tiki bar vibes, and the occasional cruise ship passing close by. These are the go-to pre-cruise dinner spots for a reason. The full Cocoa Beach dining strip — Ron Jon, Coconuts, Slow & Low — is 5 miles south down A1A.

Can I stay the night before AND the night after my cruise?

Yes. Most Sandcastles owners are happy to accommodate back-to-back nights — one the evening before embarkation and one after disembarkation. Contact the owner directly through the Airbnb or VRBO listing to arrange check-in and check-out times that work with your cruise schedule. Arriving the night before means a relaxed embarkation morning with no travel stress. Staying the night after means clearing customs, Ubering back to the condo, and spending a few more hours on the beach before the drive home. It's the cleanest possible cruise travel setup.
