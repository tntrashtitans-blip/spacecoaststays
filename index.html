Activities & Adventure

Things to Do in Cocoa Beach

From surfing the Atlantic to kayaking through wildlife refuges — the Space Coast delivers.

Full activity guide →

Surfing Lessons

0.4 mi from Sandcastles

Cocoa Beach Surf School offers beginner-friendly lessons right on the beach. Cocoa Beach is one of Florida's best surfing spots year-round.

Sport Fishing Charters

5 mi · Port Canaveral

Port Canaveral is home to a fleet of fishing charters targeting mahi-mahi, snapper, grouper, and more. Half-day and full-day offshore trips available year-round.

Kayaking — Thousand Islands

10 min from Sandcastles

Paddle through the Thousand Islands Conservation Area in the Indian River Lagoon — one of the most biodiverse estuaries in the US. Manatees, dolphins, and sea birds along the way.

Kennedy Space Center

12 mi · 20 min north

Walk beneath a Saturn V rocket, see the Space Shuttle Atlantis up close, and catch a live or simulated launch experience. One of Florida's most popular attractions for all ages.

Beach Biking on A1A

Rentals near Sandcastles

Rent cruiser bikes and ride A1A along the ocean — north toward Canaveral National Seashore or south past the Cocoa Beach Pier. Flat terrain, ocean breezes, and no car needed.

Dolphin & Sunset Cruises

Port Canaveral · 5 mi

Evening dolphin tours and sunset cruises depart from Port Canaveral. Watch bottlenose dolphins play in the wake as the sun drops over the Indian River Lagoon. A Space Coast must-do.

Paddleboarding

Rentals on the beach

Stand-up paddleboard rentals are available along A1A. Calm waters on the lagoon side are ideal for beginners, while the Atlantic side offers more of a challenge for advanced paddlers.

Lori Wilson Park

0.3 mi · Walk

A hidden gem just steps from Sandcastles. Winding nature boardwalk through coastal scrub habitat, leading to one of the cleanest stretches of beach in Cocoa Beach. Bring the dog.

Jet Ski & Boat Rentals

Port Canaveral · 5 mi

Rent a jet ski, pontoon boat, or wave runner from Port Canaveral marinas and explore the open waters of Banana River, Indian River, or the Atlantic inlet. No boating license required for rentals.

Sailing from Port Canaveral?

Port Canaveral is the second-busiest cruise port in the U.S. — and it's a 10-minute Uber from Sandcastles. Skip the overpriced port hotels: park beachside, spend a night on the Atlantic, and head to the terminal rested. Explore Beyond the Beach

Day Trips Within an Hour

Sandcastles puts you at the center of Central Florida's best — theme parks, nature, and history within easy reach.

Kennedy Space Center

12 miles · 20 minutes

NASA's working launch facility and visitor complex. See Space Shuttle Atlantis, walk under a Saturn V, and catch a rocket launch if timing is right.

Walt Disney World

58 miles · ~60 minutes

Take FL-528 West straight into Orlando. Four theme parks, Disney Springs, and resorts — a full day trip that's surprisingly easy from Cocoa Beach.

Universal Studios

55 miles · ~55 minutes

Harry Potter, Jurassic World, Epic Universe, and the rest of Universal's Orlando complex. About the same drive time as Disney but often a shorter wait.

Brevard Zoo

30 miles · 35 minutes

One of Florida's best zoos — you can kayak through the exhibits. Giraffe feeding, zip lines over wildlife, and surprisingly uncrowded compared to Orlando parks.

Port Canaveral

5 miles · 10 minutes

Watch cruise ships pass, eat fresh shrimp off the docks, and book a sport fishing charter. The best fresh seafood on the Space Coast is right here.

Canaveral National Seashore

20 miles · 25 minutes

24 miles of pristine, undeveloped Atlantic beach. No crowds, no condos — just sea turtles, shorebirds, and some of the cleanest water on Florida's east coast.

Sea Turtle Nesting Season

May – Oct · Cocoa Beach

Cocoa Beach is a major sea turtle nesting corridor. Loggerhead and green turtles nest on the beach at night from May through October. Guided night walks available locally.

Orlando Premium Outlets

55 miles · ~55 minutes

Two massive outlet centers near I-4 in Orlando — Vineland and International Drive locations. Worth the drive for a rainy day or when the surf is flat.

Common Questions

Everything You Need to Know

Answers to the most common questions about staying at Sandcastles Condominiums, Cocoa Beach FL.

Can you see rocket launches from Sandcastles?

Yes — Sandcastles is 12 miles south of Kennedy Space Center's launch pads. Upper-floor units with north-facing balconies have a clear sightline. SpaceX Falcon 9 launches are the most frequent and produce a brilliant visible arc. No need to leave the balcony.

How far is Sandcastles from the Cocoa Beach Pier?

The Cocoa Beach Pier is approximately 0.5 miles south — a 10-minute walk along the beach. The pier has multiple restaurants, a surf shop, beach bars with live music, and a 800-foot fishing deck over the Atlantic.

Is Sandcastles Condominiums directly on the beach?

Yes. The building sits directly on the Atlantic Ocean at 1000 N Atlantic Ave. Ground-floor units have a short walk to the water; upper floors have balconies overlooking the beach. The heated pool and hot tub are steps from the sand.

Are there dog-friendly units at Sandcastles?

Unit 312 (listed on Airbnb) welcomes dogs under 30 lbs. Some other owners may also allow pets — always check the individual listing policy before booking. Lori Wilson Park, 0.3 miles away, has dog-friendly beach access.

How far is Sandcastles from Disney World?

About 58 miles west — roughly 60 minutes via FL-528 (the Beachline Expressway) with no traffic. It's a legitimate day trip: leave early, spend the day at the parks, and be back for sunset on the balcony.

What restaurants are walkable from Sandcastles?

Coconuts on the Beach is a 0.4-mile walk — a classic Cocoa Beach beachfront bar with food and live music. The Cocoa Beach Pier restaurants are 0.5 miles south. For fresh seafood, Port Canaveral is a 10-minute drive and worth every minute.

What is there to do in Cocoa Beach on a rainy day?

Kennedy Space Center is fully indoors and could easily fill a whole day. Ron Jon Surf Shop is a 24-hour shopping experience. The Orlando outlets are 55 miles away. Brevard Zoo works in light rain. And Port Canaveral's seafood restaurants are always open.

Is Cocoa Beach good for families with kids?

Absolutely. The calm sections of beach near Sandcastles are great for young swimmers. Kennedy Space Center has hands-on exhibits for all ages. Brevard Zoo's kayak-through experience is a kids' favorite. And the surf is rideable — Cocoa Beach Surf School does kids' lessons.
