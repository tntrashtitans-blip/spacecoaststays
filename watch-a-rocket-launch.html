Kennedy Space Center · 12 Miles Away

Watch a Rocket Launch

Staying at Sandcastles puts you front-row for one of the most extraordinary experiences on Earth. SpaceX Falcon 9s, ULA Atlas Vs, and NASA missions all launch from Kennedy Space Center — visible right from your balcony.

Why the Space Coast

The best seats aren't at KSC

Kennedy Space Center charges for viewing access — and puts you close to the pad but packed in a crowd. From Sandcastles' upper floors, you get an unobstructed view of the rocket clearing the pad, the arc across the sky, and the sonic boom rolling in seconds later.

Night launches are particularly spectacular — the engine glow lights up the Atlantic and reflects off the water. Guests who've watched from a seventh-floor balcony say it's the highlight of their entire trip.

Best Viewing Spots

Ranked by how easy they are to reach from Sandcastles.

Best Option · 0 miles

Your Sandcastles Balcony

Upper-floor oceanfront units have a clear northwest sightline toward LC-39 and SLC-40. No traffic, no crowds — just step outside with your drink.

Easy Walk · 0 miles

The Beach at Sandcastles

Walk 100 feet to the sand and face north. Wide open sky, no buildings blocking your view. Great for photos with the ocean in frame.

Short Drive · 6 miles

Jetty Park, Port Canaveral

The closest public land to the launchpads outside of KSC. Free parking, a beach, and a direct sightline to both launch complexes. Get there 30 min early.

Official · 12 miles

KSC Visitor Complex

Paid launch viewing tickets give you access to their official viewing site and countdown commentary. Worth it once — especially if paired with a day at the complex.

Launch Day Tips

Things to know before the countdown.

📅

Launches Scrub — A Lot

Weather, technical issues, and range conflicts cause delays. Always treat a launch date as tentative and check 24 hours before your viewing plan.

📱

Get Alerts

Download Space Launch Now or follow @SpaceX and @NASA_Kennedy on X. You'll get real-time updates if a launch is delayed or scrubbed.

🌙

Night Launches Are Magical

If there's a night launch during your stay, rearrange your evening around it. The glow, the reflection on the water, and the boom overhead are unforgettable.

📷

Camera Settings

Use a tripod. For night launches: ISO 800, f/5.6, 2–4 sec shutter. Day: burst mode. Video on your phone actually works well for the arc and sonic boom.

⏱️

The Sonic Boom

If a booster returns to land at Cape Canaveral, expect a double boom 6–8 minutes after launch. It's louder than you expect. Windows rattle. Totally normal.

👟

Jetty Park Parking

For Jetty Park, arrive at least 30–45 minutes early. Parking fills fast for popular missions. Rideshare out and walk back if you want the best spot.

Plan Your Trip

Check the Launch Schedule

SpaceX, ULA, and NASA all publish upcoming launch windows. Time your stay around a mission that excites you — Crew Dragon, Starlink, or a rare NASA mission.
