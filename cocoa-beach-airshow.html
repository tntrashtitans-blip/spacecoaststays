About the Event

Florida's Space Coast Air Show

The Cocoa Beach Air Show — also billed as the Space Coast Air Show — is held each November at Patrick Space Force Base, an active military installation that borders the Atlantic coast of Cocoa Beach. The show features some of the most capable aircraft in the American arsenal alongside world-class civilian aerobatic teams and rare warbird demonstrations.

Because Patrick SFB sits directly on the ocean, the flight line runs parallel to the coastline — meaning the entire public beach becomes a grandstand. The aircraft perform their maneuvers over the water, giving spectators on the shore a perfect broadside view of every pass and high-energy pull. No ticket. No base access required. Just a spot on the sand.

The show typically draws more than 100,000 spectators over the weekend, making it one of the largest annual events on the Space Coast. Practice flights on Thursday and Friday before the main weekend give early arrivals a preview — and a significantly less crowded beach.

What to Expect

  • ✈️
    Military Demonstrations

    High-performance military jets including F/A-18s, F-35s, and A-10 Thunderbolts performing low-altitude passes and precision maneuvers.

  • 🏁
    Aerobatic Teams

    Civilian aerobatic champions and specialty acts bringing edge-of-seat knife-edge passes, lomcevaks, and ribbon cuts over the beach crowd.

  • ✈
    Warbird Demonstrations

    Restored WWII-era aircraft — P-51 Mustangs, B-17s, T-6 Texans — flying formation passes and solo routines along the shore.

  • 🚌
    Base Access & Static Displays

    On-base static aircraft displays and exhibits are available to military ID holders and sponsored guests. The flight demonstrations are visible from the public beach without base access.

What You Need to Know

Planning your air show weekend on the Space Coast.

📅

Show Dates

The Cocoa Beach Air Show is typically held over a weekend in November. Exact 2026 dates have not yet been announced — check the official Space Coast Air Show website for confirmed scheduling once it is published.

One thing worth knowing: practice flights happen the Thursday and Friday before the main weekend. If you're staying for the full run, you'll catch the aircraft from the first day of rehearsals through Sunday's finale. Early-week arrivals also get the best beach real estate before the weekend crowds arrive on A1A.

🏖️

Beach Viewing

The best free viewing runs along the Cocoa Beach shoreline between Lori Wilson Park (1500 N Atlantic Ave) and the Cocoa Beach Pier. The flight path runs parallel to the coast, so anywhere along this stretch puts you broadside to the action.

Bring sunscreen, plenty of water, and get your spot early — the beach fills up significantly by mid-morning on show days. Umbrellas and low beach chairs work well; avoid tall canopies that block the view for those behind you. Binoculars are a nice addition for tracking fast passes far down the flight line.

📍

Getting There

Sandcastles guests: Walk to the beach — no car needed. The building's beach access puts you on the sand in minutes.

Everyone else: A1A gets severely gridlocked on show weekend. Arrive early and plan to park and walk. The two best public lots are Sidney Fischer Park (2200 N Atlantic Ave) and Lori Wilson Park (1500 N Atlantic Ave). Both are within a short walk of excellent viewing spots along the flight line. Plan for a 30-minute early arrival minimum — 60 minutes on the main show days.

Reserve Before Show Weekend Sells Out

Air show weekend is one of the most popular booking periods of the year on the Space Coast. Oceanfront units at Sandcastles — especially upper-floor balcony rentals with the best sightlines south toward Patrick SFB — typically book out months in advance. If you're planning around the show, don't wait until October.

Frequently Asked Questions

Cocoa Beach Air Show FAQ

Everything you need to plan your air show trip to the Space Coast.

Where is the Cocoa Beach Air Show held?

The Cocoa Beach Air Show (also known as the Space Coast Air Show) is held at Patrick Space Force Base in Cocoa Beach, FL 32931. Patrick SFB is an active military installation situated directly on the Atlantic coast. Because the base sits on the ocean, the flight demonstrations take place along the shoreline — making the public beach one of the best free viewing areas for the show. The base's location on the barrier island means there is no high ground blocking views from the sand.

Is the Cocoa Beach Air Show free to attend?

Watching the air show from the public beach is completely free — no ticket, no registration, and no base access required. The aircraft perform their maneuvers over the Atlantic Ocean along the coastline, and the entire public shoreline from Lori Wilson Park south to the Cocoa Beach Pier provides excellent, unobstructed viewing. Access to Patrick Space Force Base itself requires a valid military ID or sponsored access. Static aircraft displays and events held on-base are restricted to those with base access credentials.

When is the Cocoa Beach Air Show in 2026?

The show is typically scheduled for a weekend in November. Official 2026 dates have not yet been confirmed at the time of this writing — check the Space Coast Air Show's official website for the published schedule once announced. Keep in mind that practice flights are usually held on the Thursday and Friday preceding the main show weekend, so arriving mid-week gives you an extended experience. Planning your travel around the Thursday through Sunday window ensures you catch every day of flight operations.

Where is the best place to watch the Cocoa Beach Air Show?

The best free viewing is along the Cocoa Beach public shoreline, with the stretch between Lori Wilson Park (1500 N Atlantic Ave) and the Cocoa Beach Pier offering the most unobstructed sightlines along the flight corridor. The flight path runs parallel to the coast, so the aircraft pass broadside in front of spectators standing on the sand. For an elevated perspective, upper-floor oceanfront condo balconies — particularly those with a southern exposure toward Patrick SFB — rank among the best vantage points available. Sandcastles Condominiums at 1000 N Atlantic Ave is approximately 2 miles from the flight line, and the aircraft fly directly over and in front of the property throughout the weekend.

Can you see the airshow from Sandcastles Condominiums?

Yes. Sandcastles Condominiums at 1000 N Atlantic Ave, Cocoa Beach is approximately 2 miles from the Patrick Space Force Base flight line. Military jets, civilian aerobatic teams, and warbird aircraft fly directly over and in front of the property during both Thursday–Friday practice days and the main show weekend. Upper-floor oceanfront units have unobstructed balcony sightlines south toward Patrick SFB. Guests can watch from their private balcony or walk to the beach in minutes — no car, no parking, no waiting in traffic on A1A when the show wraps up.
