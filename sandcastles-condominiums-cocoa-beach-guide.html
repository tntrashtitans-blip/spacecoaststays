Sandcastles Condominiums: what to know before booking.

Sandcastles is the current center of SpaceCoastStays: individually owned Cocoa Beach condos with direct booking paths through Airbnb and VRBO.

Stay Guide / Sandcastles

Why guests choose Sandcastles

Sandcastles is for travelers who want Cocoa Beach to feel easy. You are staying directly on the Atlantic, close to the sand, close to restaurants, and close enough to Port Canaveral and Kennedy Space Center that the rest of the Space Coast still feels within reach.

The big difference from a hotel is space. A Sandcastles condo gives you a kitchen, living room, separate bedrooms, a private balcony, and a place to settle in after the beach. That matters for families, snowbirds, cruise travelers adding beach time, and launch watchers who may be waiting on a moving countdown.

Each unit has its own personality

Sandcastles is not a single hotel inventory where every room is basically the same. The condos are individually owned, so one unit may be freshly renovated and dog-friendly, another may have a better direct ocean view, and another may be a stronger value for a monthly stay.

That variety is the reason SpaceCoastStays exists. The goal is to help you compare real listings in one place, then send you directly to the Airbnb or VRBO page where the owner or manager handles availability, pricing, rules, and booking.

What to compare before choosing a unit

  • View type: direct oceanfront, ocean view, pool view, or side view.
  • Floor level and balcony photos.
  • Bed setup, bathrooms, laundry, kitchen equipment, and beach gear.
  • Pet policy, parking notes, monthly stay terms, and platform fees.
  • Whether the unit has a live iCal calendar connected on SpaceCoastStays.

How to browse Sandcastles units

Start with the Browse Units tab. That page shows individual Sandcastles condos as picture listings, so you can compare the living space, balcony views, floor level, bedroom setup, and booking platform before opening Airbnb or VRBO.

Some listings include a unit number and floor when that information is known. You can also filter by view type, such as direct oceanfront, ocean view, or pool view. The photos still matter because every condo sits differently in the building and each owner presents the space a little differently.

Some featured units also have live calendar links connected to the date search. If one or more of those units are open for the dates you search, they move to the top of the page with an availability notice. Units without a connected calendar remain browseable, but their availability still needs to be confirmed on the Airbnb or VRBO listing page.

Who Sandcastles is best for

Sandcastles is a good fit for guests who want more than a hotel room. Families can use the kitchen and separate bedrooms. Snowbirds can settle into a routine. Launch watchers can choose a beach base. Cruise travelers can add a real beach stay before or after Port Canaveral instead of only sleeping near the terminal.

It may not be the right fit for someone who wants a full-service resort with one front desk managing every room. The strength of Sandcastles is the condo setup: different views, different styles, different owners, and enough variety for guests to choose what fits their trip.

Booking tip

Use SpaceCoastStays to compare units and narrow your choices. Before booking, open the Airbnb or VRBO listing and confirm the current rate, fees, cancellation rules, pet policy, and host terms directly on that platform.

Best next step

Go to Browse Units, use the view and platform filters, and check dates if your trip depends on availability.

Compare the actual Sandcastles listings

Use the Sandcastles directory to compare views, floors, booking platforms, photos, and live availability for featured units. Then book directly through the owner-managed Airbnb or VRBO listing.
