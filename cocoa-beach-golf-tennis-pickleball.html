Active Stays

Golf, tennis, and pickleball near Sandcastles.

A Cocoa Beach stay does not have to be only beach chairs and seafood dinners. Sandcastles works well for guests who want an active week with tennis on site, golf nearby, and pickleball options around the Space Coast.

Stay Guide / Golf, Tennis & Pickleball

Tennis is the easiest activity at Sandcastles

For guests staying at Sandcastles, tennis is the simplest active option because the courts are part of the property. That makes it easy to play before the beach gets hot, squeeze in a casual match before dinner, or keep a longer winter stay from feeling too slow.

If tennis matters to your group, compare units for convenience too. A condo with a comfortable living room, laundry, and beach gear makes it easier to mix court time with pool time and beach days without turning the trip into logistics.

Golf near Cocoa Beach

Cocoa Beach Country Club is the closest practical golf option for many Sandcastles guests. It gives visitors a way to add a morning round without losing the whole day to driving. For guests willing to go farther, Space Coast courses around Viera and Melbourne can create a more golf-focused day trip.

Golf is especially useful for snowbirds and monthly guests because it adds rhythm to a longer stay. A few rounds across a month can balance beach time, visiting family, and quieter days around the condo.

Pickleball around the Space Coast

Pickleball availability can change by season, facility schedule, and local programming, so guests should check current court times before building a trip around it. Still, Cocoa Beach and the surrounding Space Coast are active enough that pickleball can be part of a winter or family stay if you are willing to drive a bit.

Best fit for active guests

  • Stay at Sandcastles if you want tennis, beach, pool, and condo space in one base.
  • Add Cocoa Beach golf when you want an active morning without leaving town for the day.
  • Look across the wider Space Coast for more golf and pickleball variety.
  • Choose a condo with laundry and a comfortable layout if you plan to be active most days.

Activity planning tip

For longer stays, think in routines: beach walk, tennis, pool, golf morning, launch night, quiet condo day. Sandcastles works because those choices can rotate without moving your home base.
