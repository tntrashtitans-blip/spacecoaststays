Why Snowbirds Choose Cocoa Beach

Cocoa Beach sits on a narrow barrier island on Florida's east coast — 60 miles from Orlando, a world away from the crowds. January highs average 68°F with almost no frost, and the Atlantic breezes keep even the warmest winter days comfortable. Unlike South Florida, Cocoa Beach still feels like a real beach town: walkable, uncrowded, and affordable, with a local community rather than a tourist machine. Add direct beach access, a full calendar of rocket launches, wildlife refuges, and a marina packed with fresh seafood, and it's easy to understand why the Space Coast has become one of Florida's best-kept snowbird secrets.

Practical Living Near Sandcastles

Everything you need for daily life is close. Here's what's nearby and how far.

Groceries & Daily Shopping

Publix on N Orlando Ave ~2-3 miles. Winn-Dixie ~5 miles on Merritt Island. Port Canaveral fish markets 6 miles north — buy fresh grouper and shrimp straight off the boat. Everything you need for a full season is covered.

Healthcare & Emergency Services

Health First Cape Canaveral Hospital ~3 miles (701 W Cocoa Beach Cswy). Urgent care clinics throughout Brevard County. CVS and Walgreens within 5 miles for prescriptions — transfers are easy for seasonal residents.

Safety & City Services

Cocoa Beach runs its own city police force — not just county coverage. Fire Station 61 is within 1 mile. A tight-knit city of ~11,000 residents: walkable, low crime, well-maintained.

Getting Around — Bike-Friendly by Design

You don't need a car for daily life in Cocoa Beach. Most seasonal residents rely on bikes for everything except day trips.

  • Dedicated multi-use path on A1A — a wide sidewalk and shared-use trail runs the full length of A1A through Cocoa Beach, completely separated from traffic. It connects parks, restaurants, shops, and the beach without touching a road lane.
  • Beach riding at low tide — cycling on the hard-packed sand at low tide is permitted on Cocoa Beach. It's a distinctly Florida experience and one of the best ways to cover a few miles of coastline before breakfast.
  • Flat and compact — Cocoa Beach has essentially no elevation change. A bike handles grocery runs, restaurant trips, beach access, and most errands without breaking a sweat. The entire main commercial strip is within easy cycling range of Sandcastles.
  • Bike rentals available locally — if you don't want to ship your own, local rental options are available in town. Many seasonal residents bring their own bike; others pick one up locally for the season.
  • A car helps for day trips — Kennedy Space Center, Port Canaveral, Orlando, and the rest of Brevard County are worth exploring by car. But for the daily rhythm of life during your stay, two wheels are plenty.
  • Ron Jon Surf Shop rents beach cruisers by the hour or day — pick one up if guests need a second set of wheels.

Parks & Outdoor Life

Cocoa Beach punches well above its size when it comes to parks. The entire shoreline is public — a daily sunrise walk is part of the snowbird lifestyle here.

Sidney Fischer Park

0.3 mi from Sandcastles

Your closest public beach access point. Picnic areas, restrooms, outdoor showers, and ample parking. Free to use. Great for morning walks or afternoon sessions on the sand — close enough that you won't need to drive.

Lori Wilson Park

1.3 mi from Sandcastles

One of the finest parks on the entire Space Coast. A nature boardwalk winds through coastal scrub habitat — a rare and protected ecosystem — before opening onto a gorgeous stretch of beach. Perfect for birding, quiet walks, and watching the shore birds work the surf. Dogs welcome on leash.

Alan Shepard Park

Near the Cocoa Beach Pier

Named after Alan Shepard, the first American in space — who was a Cocoa Beach resident. Beach access, parking, and restrooms near the iconic Cocoa Beach Pier. A good starting point for a pier walk or a sunset stroll down the beach.

Jetty Park

Port Canaveral · 6 mi

A gem worth the short drive. Walking trails, a long fishing pier, beach access, and picnic areas — plus a front-row view of cruise ships departing Port Canaveral and an unobstructed line of sight to the launch pads. Pack lunch and spend a morning here.

The Entire Shoreline

From your front door

Every inch of Cocoa Beach's Atlantic shoreline is public. There are no private beach segments, no hotel-only sections. Walk as far north or south as you like. A daily sunrise walk — coffee optional but recommended — becomes the anchor of the snowbird morning routine within the first week.

Golf on the Space Coast

Brevard County has more than a dozen courses within 30 minutes. If golf is part of your winter routine, you won't run out of options.

Cocoa Beach Country Club

~1 mile

An 18-hole municipal course that sits practically in Sandcastles' backyard — motivated golfers can bike there. One of the most affordable oceanside courses in Florida, with a genuine local atmosphere. No tee-time drama, no resort pricing. This is where Cocoa Beach residents actually play.

Duran Golf Club

~20 miles · Viera

A well-regarded public course in the fast-growing Viera community. Good conditioning, fair pricing, and a more formal course experience. A solid choice when you want something a step up from the municipal layout.

Viera East Golf Club

~25 miles · Viera

A championship-level public course with excellent practice facilities. Popular with serious golfers making the trip from the beach for a full round. Worth combining with errands in Viera or a visit to the Brevard Zoo nearby.

More Brevard Courses

Within 30 min

Brevard County has a dozen courses within a 30-minute drive of Cocoa Beach — from casual nine-hole layouts to championship tracks. Many are municipal or semi-private with reasonable green fees. Ask locals at the Cocoa Beach Country Club for current recommendations.

Things to Do All Winter Long

A season on the Space Coast means more than beach days. Winter is actually the best time to be here for many of the region's signature experiences.

Rocket Launches

Multiple times/month · Watch from balcony

SpaceX launches from Cape Canaveral year-round, with November through March delivering some of the clearest viewing conditions of the year — low humidity and cool, transparent air. Launch events draw locals and visitors to the beach and Jetty Park for what amounts to a spontaneous block party. Watch from your balcony or walk to the shoreline.

Kennedy Space Center

12 miles · 20 min north

Many snowbirds who visit KSC once end up going back two or three more times during the season. The exhibits change, the live programs vary, and the experience of standing under a Saturn V rocket never really gets old. Buy an annual pass and treat it like a local.

Manatee & Wildlife Season

Oct–Mar · Indian River Lagoon

November through March is peak manatee season in Florida. Blue Spring State Park (90 minutes from Cocoa Beach) is one of the best places in the world to see manatees up close. On cool mornings, hundreds gather in the warm spring water. An easy day trip and a genuinely unforgettable experience.

Space Coast Birding Festival

January · Titusville

Winter is prime migratory season at Merritt Island National Wildlife Refuge — one of the most biodiverse refuges in the United States, just 15 minutes from Sandcastles. Roseate spoonbills, bald eagles, herons, sandpipers, and hundreds of migratory species crowd the impoundments and shorelines. Birders from around the country make special trips here in January and February.

Port Canaveral Seafood

5 miles · Grills & Rusty's

The waterfront at Port Canaveral has a cluster of seafood restaurants — Grills, Rusty's, and others — where the catch comes straight off the boats docked nearby. Grouper, shrimp, oysters, stone crab when in season. Make it a weekly ritual; the sunsets from the marina are worth the trip on their own.

Day Trips from the Beach

KSC · Orlando · St. Augustine

Orlando is an hour away — theme parks, outlet shopping, major airports. St. Augustine (2 hours north) is the oldest city in the US and worth a two-day trip. Tampa (2 hours west) offers Busch Gardens, the Riverwalk, and Ybor City. Cocoa Beach sits in one of Florida's most central locations for exploring the state.
